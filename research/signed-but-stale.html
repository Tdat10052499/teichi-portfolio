Signed but Stale.

Rolling Back Backdoor Repairs Through Shard-Version Skew in 6G Edge Inference

The question

This work examines the relationship between signed updates, shard-version consistency and backdoor repairs in distributed edge inference. The central question: can older shard versions undermine a repair even when updates carry valid signatures?
